Riley's Gambit

Roll a die. Whatever number it lands on is now in your House. Roll again. If you roll a number that's touching that number (one above or one below), add it to your House. The game ends when you roll a number that's not touching any of the numbers in your House (lose), or when you collect all 6 numbers in your House (win).
The numbers loop around, so 6 and 1 are touching. If you win without relying on the 6–1 loop, that's a natural win.
Use the manual roller to play one roll at a time, or the batch sequencer to run games instantly. Every game is logged in the sidebar — click to expand, or replay wins on the house ring.
