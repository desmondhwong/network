Colophon

This page is an homage to Laurel Schwulst’s Instructions for a Typeface (now offline), retrieved via the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine.

Several prompts also carry a quiet thread from an essay outline by cathschmidt: on imperialism, typography, and the technical constraints of printing and computing—especially the ways “support” and “standards” can disguise imposed defaults.

Implementation: a finite deck of 52 prompts is shuffled; “Next” walks the deck without repeats, then reshuffles.
