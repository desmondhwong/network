HLC field notes

Sources and editorial approach

This is a 34-page explanatory blog authored for the Sitemap 0.3 demonstration on 16 September 2026. It adapts the user-requested local HLC corpus at codex/projects/hlc into a connected introduction. The original notes remain in their workspace; this folder is a new derived artifact, not a replacement, release, or authoritative edition of them.

All body text here is newly written paraphrase or editorial explanation. The library examples and the compact exercises are illustrative adaptations, not reports of experiments performed for the original project. Machine provenance appears in an HTML comment at the start of every Markdown page. The home page describes the intended reading experience, and reading paths offer optional routes through the material.

The local codex/projects/hlc/hypertext/SUMMARY.md was used as a finding aid to the corpus. Its summaries were not treated as a substitute for the selected source passages or as governance over this derived blog.

What this edition changes

The sources mix conceptual proposals, conversation, technical assertions, critical interpretation, and speculative synthesis. This guide preserves the central vocabulary while separating those registers. Claims that a prompt directly measures latent geometry, that a generated idea is unprecedented, or that a symbolic experience proves an ontology are presented as claims requiring evidence rather than as established results. The evidence page makes that editorial standard explicit.

The astronomical and holographic terms are used as organizing metaphors unless a specific formal mechanism is supplied. The blog’s own graph represents authored hyperlinks between pages. It is not an empirical visualization of model internals. No external research, current product survey, or experimental validation is claimed for this adaptation.
