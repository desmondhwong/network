Help

Choose 1–100 pairs and start a round. One board comes from a Black win, the other from a White win. Choose the game won by the color in the question. Results and identities stay hidden until you answer. The round stops at your chosen count, including timed-out pairs, and gives you a summary.

These are the last positions in the records. A resigned game may still have unsettled territory; the recorded winner is the answer. This exercise does not calculate the score or evaluate a position with an AI. After each answer, open either game to see the moves and source.

Use A/B or the arrow keys to answer, N for the next pair, Space to pause, and comma for controls. Opening another view or hiding the tab pauses an active round. The statistics below the boards describe the current round; Session groups rounds and keeps the last 2,000 answers in this browser. Immediate auto-next shows the answer highlight for one frame before advancing.
